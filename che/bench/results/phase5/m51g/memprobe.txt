A candidate that fits is not automatically the right choice: remat
preserves the experiment exactly (same hyperparameters, same
updates, loss verified identical), while n_minibatches / pop_size /
n_envs change what is being run. That trade is a human call.

Against a 31.8 GiB card:
  fits: remat+nmb8, nmb8, nmb16, pop6, envs128
  no experiment-preserving option fits: every candidate that
  fits alters n_minibatches, pop_size or n_envs. HUMAN RULING
  REQUIRED — do not pick one to make the gate pass.

Headroom matters as much as fitting: a config that needs ~95 % of the
card is not a safe operating point for a 14-run grid.
