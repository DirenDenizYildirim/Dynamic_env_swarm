How to use this (ruling 3b): falsifier condition (i) reads 'within the
measured reproducibility floor', citing this study. A cross-arm effect
smaller than ~2x the sd here is NOT resolvable at one run per cell.

CAVEAT, stated because it bounds every use: n = 4 gives an sd with
3 degrees of freedom — itself uncertain by roughly +/-40 %. Treat
the floor as an order of magnitude, not a threshold to three decimals.
It also describes the NON-DETERMINISTIC regime; if headline runs go
deterministic (ruling 1d/3c), their floor is a different, smaller
quantity dominated by seed variation and must be measured separately.
