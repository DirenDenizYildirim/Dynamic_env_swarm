# FINDINGS — the disposition of every finding (builder B1, step 3)

Written 2026-10-11 under PAPER V2 (`docs/decision_log.md:6036`) and its
amendment. One row per finding: the owner's fix list, OWNER ITEMS 1–6, the
`KIT_BUILD.md` differences, the step-2 review's N1–N17, its ruling-level
decisions and record items, every new finding in the three plans, and the
orchestrator's O1. Step-4 review lanes **append** their outcomes in §4;
nothing above §4 is edited after step 3 except by the orchestrator.

**Classes (the orchestrator's policy):**
- **A, owner-directed**: on the owner's fix list; applied as the owner
  directed.
- **B, standing rule**: not on the list, but the demo or the owner's text
  breaks a standing written rule (the rules of `paper_v2_prompt.md:66-91`;
  `branch_B.md` §5 and §8; the registered strings; "every claim traces to
  the record"); applied, and the provenance row says "class B, finding
  <ID>" with the original wording, so the owner can revert.
- **C, needs a ruling or an owner choice**: not applied; v2 keeps the
  default that needs no ruling; the finding goes to the owner with its
  location and proposed direction; where a choice needs it, an alternate is
  drafted in `sections/alt/`.

**ID prefixes:** M, C, F = fix list; OI = OWNER ITEMS; KB = `KIT_BUILD.md`
differences; LEN = the fix list's length item; N = the review's new
findings; RV-R = the review's ruling-level decisions; REC = the review's
record items (§1.3); P1-, P2-, P3- = the plans' findings; O1 = the
orchestrator. "Applied by" names the writer (W1–W6), B2, or "owner
decision". Locations are the demo's, file-local (`sections/NN_*.md:line`)
unless a path is given.
