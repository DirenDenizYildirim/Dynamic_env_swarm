### Move 3: why (why the correction exists, *not* why the gain exists)

- **Content.**
  - Communication denial is inert under the frozen random-projection message
    encoder at this scale (§7; COM-4, ENV-12).
  - So a third of ISO's training episodes carried no active stressor (DES-8).
    They are fire-only, not hazard-free (`writing_session_prompt.md:161`).
  - **Owned in one plain clause as a design error** (F3; §4 below gives the
    record).
  - A 20-seed sensitivity arm, ISO-4, outside the confirmatory family (DES-13,
    DES-15), removes those episodes. Its survival is 0.98 points above ISO's
    (RES-7; **an estimate, with no verdict word**, FIGURE BATCH B7).
  - JOINT then differs from ISO-4 by −0.03 points, CI [−0.68, +0.63] (RES-6),
    although JOINT still sees each coupling twice as often and always in
    combination (DES-14).
- **Must not.**
  - Attribute the gain to the active share, or to fire-only training. B̂ has no
    test. ISO → ISO-4 changes the active share (2/3 → 1) and each coupling's
    frequency (1/3 → 1/2) together (DES-14). BRIEF A8 sets the ceiling
    (`paper/demo_beyond/BRIEF.md:130`).
  - Use "raises" as a verdict. "Removing them raises survival by 0.98"
    (`finale.md:13`) states an estimate as if it carried a verdict. Prefer the
    estimate form ("ISO-4's survival is 0.98 points above ISO's"). This is a
    word-level choice for the owner.
  - Call δ "certified inert" (`writing_session_prompt.md:158-159`).
  - Present the registration as foresight (§4).
- **Optional.** One clause saying what remains open: the correction excludes a
  large composition effect, not every one (RES-6's +0.63 lies above RES-2's
  +0.47). The CI already carries this, so the clause is optional at abstract
  level. It is required in §1 and §9.2.

### Move 4: what the environment and the method contribute

- **Content.**
  - CHE: pure JAX, multi-agent; a cellular-automaton fire in every episode;
    collapse can add ignitions (Coupling A); smoke attenuates perception by
    Beer–Lambert transmittance (Coupling B); an independent comms-denial axis.
  - The fire is an **ambient hazard**: no reward, cost or constraint channel
    reads it. The reward reads task variables and deaths and prices each death
    the same (Def. 2; DEF. 2 WORDING RULING, log:5314-5323; ENV-1). This is
    demoted from the title, and kept here as the property that makes the
    comparison clean.
  - Severity is placed relative to a measured percolation threshold (CAL-3;
    the β̂_c provenance caveat, O-2, stays in §4).
  - Each stressor is a parameter of one kernel, so ablations are bitwise-exact
    nested models (`CLAUDE.md` invariant 3; `test_nesting.py`).
  - A measurement protocol whose acceptance bars come with floors, measured per
    metric, per GPU and per artifact (METH-1, METH-2). It describes the
    protocol, not universal compliance: §8.6 names the bars without floors, and
    HANDOFF.md:157-159 records the spine's contradicted "every acceptance bar"

### What this means for the owner's own F3 wording (finding N1; report before fixing)

The owner's F3 says δ "proved inert", that the design "did not anticipate
this", and that the error "was found and registered … mid-grid"
(`paper_v2_prompt.md:169-175`). Against the record:

- **"Proved inert"** is true, but it **predates the design** (2026-07-30 vs
  2026-08-02). v2 must not imply the inertness was discovered after the
  design. The more honest point, and the stronger one, is the design's own:
