### 5.2 Rules (kit facts)

- The compile step rewrites **every lone `$` to `$$`**, in code and comments
  too (`KIT_NOTES.md:172-174`). Inline `$…$` becomes `$$…$$` inline; display
  math is its own paragraph between `$$` lines. **No `$` in raw HTML**:
  figcaptions, `<p class="caption">`, `<d-footnote>` and HTML listing
  captions take Unicode (κ<sub>B</sub>, θ&#42;).
- **No `{{` or `{%` anywhere**, math included (`x^{{2}}` runs Liquid), except
  the two figure forms (`KIT_NOTES.md:283-284`).
- Absolute values and norms: `\lvert…\rvert` and `\lVert…\rVert`;
  conditioning: `\mid`. Never `|` in inline math or prose (M1), and never `|`
  inside math in a table row.
- Never put a single inline expression alone as its own paragraph. After the
  rewrite it becomes a `$$` paragraph, which kramdown renders as display
  (builder's reading of kramdown's block-math rule, UNVERIFIED).
- The kit's MathJax handled `\underbrace`, `aligned`, `\blacksquare`,
