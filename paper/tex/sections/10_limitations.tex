\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

These apply on every outcome branch and are stated in the same order on each,
so that none is disclosed only where the result makes it convenient.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Medium siting.} $\thetastar$ is at the severity where
  Coupling B's own measured survival effect was $-0.0003$; its eight-point
  effect is at High. Medium was chosen because both couplings clear their lock
  criteria there and floors are smallest. That trades effect existence for
  measurability. By the project's own locks there is no severity at which both
  couplings are strongly behaviourally live: at Medium B is quiet and A is
  live, at High A is marginal (the supercritical fire consumes the fuel
  collapse would ignite) and B is live. The High readout adds breadth in
  severity; it does not supply a test where both elements bite.
  \item \textbf{One held-out severity}, interpolated in one scalar between the
  training severities. The near-critical regime is the test point and no
  training data lies near criticality.
  \item \textbf{The task requires no coordination.} Foraging with a team
  reward on twelve shared-parameter agents. Swarm-scale effects are a design
  choice, not a requirement of the task, and we do not use ``swarm'' as a
  claim.
  \item \textbf{The death penalty.} The reward carries a penalty on the alive
  transition (\S\ref{sec:env}). Survival is therefore a penalized quantity
  and completion a rewarded one; claims about asymmetry between them are
  scoped in those terms, and the no-penalty ablation is reported.
  \item \textbf{The communication encoder is frozen.} The message head
  receives no gradient; the encoder is a random projection. ``Inert'' is
  scoped to this encoder and this scale (\S\ref{sec:comms}).
  \item \textbf{Observations contain absolute position and time.}
  Theorem~\ref{thm:e2c} addresses memorization, but the confound is real. Its
  visible form is a positional drift: in every episode of every arm the team
  ends near a boundary. The drift channels are recorded, ungraded, and their
  floors are owed.
  \item \textbf{$\Gam$'s interval is conditional on the common evaluation
  draw} (512 episodes, one seed) and reflects training-seed variance only.
  \item \textbf{Card structure.} Every confirmatory run trained and evaluated
  on one card. The secondary sweeps ran on two cards with seed-major order,
  so both arms of any seed share a card and an additive card effect cancels
  within a seed; the block structure is reported in
  Appendix~\ref{app:provenance}. A card-by-arm interaction is not removed by
  balance, which is why the confirmatory arms were re-run on one card before
  unblinding.
  \item \textbf{The endpoint contrast confounds dose with composition.}
  \JOINT{} sees each element three times as often as \ISO{}. The matched-marginal
  sweep separates the two but is secondary and non-verdict-bearing, and it
  cannot hold the no-element share fixed at the same time.
  \item \textbf{No external baseline.} No domain-randomization schedule, no
  curriculum method. The paper compares training distributions on one
  environment.
  \item \textbf{Hazard simplicity.} Burn time one step, single ignition, no
  wind, no fuel heterogeneity; the fire is a percolation front by
  construction, which is what makes its phase measurable.
\end{enumerate}
