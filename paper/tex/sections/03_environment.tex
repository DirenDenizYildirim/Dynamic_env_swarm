\section{The environment}
\label{sec:env}

We state the formal objects compactly; proofs that are short enough to be
read inline are given inline, the rest in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}.
Throughout, $G \subset \mathbb{Z}^2$ is a grid of $m$ cells with von Neumann
neighbourhood $N(\cdot)$, $I = \{1,\dots,n\}$ indexes agents, and a
\emph{stressor configuration} is $\theta = (\beta, \kA, \kB, \delta)$:
hazard transmissibility, Coupling-A strength, Coupling-B strength and
communication-denial level.

\subsection{A factored Dec-POMDP with a fixed step order}

\begin{definition}[Compound Hostile Environment]
\label{def:che}
A \CHE{} is a Dec-POMDP $\mathcal{M}_\theta = \langle I, \mathcal{S},
\{\mathcal{A}_i\}, T_\theta, R, \{\Omega_i\}, O_\theta, \gamma\rangle$ whose
state factors as $\mathcal{S} = \mathcal{X} \times \mathcal{H} \times
\mathcal{C} \times \mathcal{K}$: joint agent state (positions, alive flags,
carried task state); the hazard field $\mathcal{H} = \{\mathrm{Fuel},
\mathrm{Burning}, \mathrm{Burnt}\}^{G}$ together with a smoke density
$\rho \in [0,\infty)^G$; the structural state $\mathcal{C} =
\{\mathrm{intact}, \mathrm{collapsed}\}^{G}$; and the realized communication
graph $\mathcal{K}$. The transition kernel factorizes as
\begin{equation}
  T_\theta(s' \mid s, a) = T_C(c' \mid c, x)\;
  T_H^{\beta,\kA}(h' \mid h, c, c')\;
  T_X(x' \mid x, a, h, c)\;
  T_K^{\delta}(k' \mid x'),
  \label{eq:factor}
\end{equation}
and each agent's observation is drawn from the \emph{post-step} state,
$O^i_{\kB}(o_i \mid x', h', \rho', c', k')$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Markov closure]
\label{prop:markov}
For every $\theta$ the process $s_t$ under any joint policy is a Markov chain
and $\mathcal{M}_\theta$ is a well-defined finite Dec-POMDP.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Each factor in \eqref{eq:factor} is a Markov kernel conditioned on components
of $(s,a)$ or on components of $s'$ already sampled earlier in the same step:
$c'$ first, then $h'$ given $(h, c, c')$, then $x'$, then $k'$ given $x'$. A
sequential composition of Markov kernels is a Markov kernel on the product
space. All spaces are finite, so finite-horizon optimal joint policies exist.
\end{proof}

The order in \eqref{eq:factor} is not an implementation detail. Coupling A
reads the collapse \emph{increment} $c' - c$, so structure must be sampled
before hazard; the smoke update $\rho' = e^{-\eta}\rho + \sigma_s
\mathbf{1}[\text{burning}]$ follows the hazard; agents move under the
pre-step hazard and structure; and the link graph is drawn from post-step
positions. The implementation performs exactly this sequence inside one
jitted step, and a unit test asserts it. Finite-horizon Dec-POMDPs are
NEXP-complete~\citep{bernstein2002}, and with $\delta > 0$ the \CHE{} does not
collapse to a multi-agent POMDP, which is the standard justification for
learning rather than planning.

\subsection{The hazard is ambient}

\begin{definition}[Ambient survival stressor]
\label{def:ambient}
The hazard is an \emph{ambient survival stressor} iff
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \emph{Reward independence.} $R$ is a function of task variables and
  of the agent-state transition only: $R(s,a,s') = R(\mathrm{task}(x), a,
  \alpha \to \alpha')$, where $\alpha$ are the alive flags. No term of $R$
  references $h$, $\rho$ or $c$, and the hazard appears in no auxiliary cost
  or constraint channel: no CMDP constraint, no Lagrangian penalty, no
  safety critic.
  \item \emph{Non-adversarial.} $T_H$ is a fixed stochastic kernel with no
  optimizing, learning or best-responding component.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

Clause~1 is the paper's boundary with two literatures. Monitoring and
suppression work places the hazard inside $R$; safe RL places it in a cost or
constraint channel. Here the hazard reaches the return only through the
transition kernel (it disables agents standing on burning cells) and the
observation kernel (Coupling B). We state one thing plainly that the phrase
``hazard-independent reward'' can hide: the training reward \emph{does}
include a penalty of $0.5$ per newly disabled agent. That term reads the
alive transition, an $\mathcal{X}$ variable, and never the hazard; it is
permitted by Clause~1, it prices a fire death and a collapse death
identically, and it matters. In the calibration phase, adding it moved
survival at the highest severity from \todo{verify 0.575} to
\todo{verify 0.866}, and completion from \todo{verify 0.765} to
\todo{verify 0.821}. The reader should therefore
understand ``the reward cannot see the hazard'' literally: it sees deaths, not
fire. A test enforces Clause~1 mechanically by constructing two states that
differ only in hazard, smoke and structure and asserting identical rewards.
Clause~2 admits one honest subtlety: through $T_C(c' \mid c, x)$ agent load
can trigger collapse and hence, via Coupling A, fire. The environment is
non-adversarial in the formal sense (fixed kernel, no objective) but not
agent-independent, which is also realistic.

\subsection{The fire and its exact phase structure}

\begin{definition}[Fire-CA kernel]
\label{def:fire}
Per cell, states $\{\mathrm{Fuel}, \mathrm{Burning}, \mathrm{Burnt}\}$. At each
step, independently for each ordered pair (Burning cell $g$, Fuel neighbour
$g' \in N(g)$), $g'$ ignites with probability $\beta$. A Burning cell becomes
Burnt after exactly one step; Burnt is absorbing. Spontaneous ignition
$\iota \ge 0$ per Fuel cell per step is a knob, set to $0$ in every result
reported here; each episode starts from a single ignition.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Exact bond-percolation equivalence]
\label{prop:perc}
Under Definition~\ref{def:fire} with $\iota = 0$ and a single ignition at the
origin, the set of eventually-Burnt cells is equal in distribution to the open
cluster of the origin in i.i.d.\ bond percolation on $(G,E)$ with edge-open
probability $\beta$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Attach to each unordered edge $e$ an independent $B_e \sim
\mathrm{Bernoulli}(\beta)$ and let a transmission attempt across $e$ succeed
iff $B_e = 1$. Each edge is attempted at most once: when one endpoint first
burns while the other is Fuel, one attempt occurs; afterwards the burning
endpoint is Burnt, and if the other endpoint later burns by another path its
neighbour across $e$ is already Burnt. The fire run with $\{B_e\}$ burns
exactly the connected component of the origin in the open subgraph.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Phase structure]
\label{cor:phase}
On $\mathbb{Z}^2$ with von Neumann neighbourhood: $\betac = 1/2$ exactly
\citep{kesten1980}; for $\beta < \betac$ the burnt cluster is almost surely
finite with mean size $\chi(\beta) < \infty$; $\chi(\beta) \sim
|\beta-\betac|^{-43/18}$ as $\beta \uparrow \betac$; for $\beta > \betac$ the
fire spreads indefinitely with positive probability, with a linear front.
\end{corollary}

This is the SIR-with-constant-infectious-period mapping of
\citet{grassberger1983}; the short proof is worth carrying because it imports
percolation theory wholesale. Exactness is specific to the idealized kernel.
The implemented kernel's critical point is \emph{measured}
(\S\ref{sec:severity}); the design relies only on the existence of the
transition and the divergence of $\chi$ near it, which are universal.

\begin{definition}[Severity by dynamical phase]
\label{def:severity}
Fix arena side $L$. \emph{Low} is subcritical ($\xi(\beta) \ll L$): clusters
are local and survival is local avoidance. \emph{Medium} is near-critical
($\xi(\beta) \sim L$): cluster sizes are finite-size scale-free and a single
ignition can cascade to arena scale. \emph{High} is supercritical: fronts
propagate at a speed comparable to agent speed and survival is a race.
\end{definition}

The three severities are three qualitatively different phases of one kernel,
which answers ``why these levels'' a priori and makes severity transfer a
statement about generalization across phases rather than interpolation
between knob values.

\subsection{Coupling A: structural failure seeds the hazard}

\begin{definition}[Coupling A]
\label{def:coupA}
Cell $g$ collapses with probability $\lambda(g, x_t, c_t) = \lambda_0 +
\lambda_{\mathrm{load}}\,\mathbf{1}[\text{agent on } g]$ if it is one of a
fraction $f_{\mathrm{weak}}$ of weak cells; collapse is absorbing. Each
collapse event at $g$ ignites each Fuel cell within radius $r_A$ of $g$
independently with probability $\kA$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Collapse events dominate exposure; divergence near criticality]
\label{prop:coupA}
In the subcritical regime with $\iota = 0$, collapse events arriving at rate
$\lambda_A$ per step and seeded clusters pairwise disjoint, the expected
burnt area by time $T$ is $\mathbb{E}[B_T] = \lambda_A T \chi(\beta)(1+o(1))$,
and in general $\mathbb{E}[B_T] \le \lambda_A T \chi(\beta)$. Hence
$\partial\,\mathbb{E}[B_T]/\partial \lambda_A = T\chi(\beta)$ diverges as
$\beta \uparrow \betac$ like $|\beta - \betac|^{-43/18}$.
\end{proposition}

In calm conditions with no spontaneous ignition, structural failure is the
hazard's only birth channel, and near criticality a single collapse has
divergent expected consequences. Four finite-protocol corrections (an
unconditional cluster-mass reference, matched seed location, matched seed age,
and sparsity) separate the equality from what a finite experiment measures;
\S\ref{sec:coupA} reports the handshake.

\subsection{Coupling B: the hazard degrades perception}

\begin{definition}[Coupling B, Beer--Lambert observation]
\label{def:coupB}
Agent $i$ observes a feature at $y$ from $x_i$ with probability equal to the
transmittance along the line of sight, $\tau_i(y) = \exp(-\kB \int_{x_i \to y}
\rho\, d\ell)$, where $\rho$ is the smoke field with emission $\sigma_s$ on
burning cells and decay $\eta$ per step. $\kB = 0$ recovers
hazard-independent observation.
\end{definition}

Smoke outlives flame by construction, so degradation persists after the front
has passed. The structural point is that $O^i$ depends on the hazard's own
state: perception fails exactly where and when the hazard is active, which is
what separates Coupling B from an always-on noise axis and what makes the
following possible.

\begin{theorem}[Memorization gap, and its erosion by Coupling B]
\label{thm:e2c}
In the two-corridor environment $E_{2C}(\kB)$ of Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}
(a $d$-step approach to a branch, two corridors of length $\ell$, zero slack,
one corridor burning at depth $\ell_f$, an informative signal at each approach
step with probability $e^{-\kB(d - t + \ell_f)}$), let $q(\kB) = 1 -
\prod_{j=\ell_f}^{d+\ell_f}(1 - e^{-\kB j})$. Then the optimal value is
$J^\ast(\kB) = \tfrac12 + \tfrac12 q(\kB)$; every policy optimal for the
fixed-map variant has value $\tfrac12$ for all $\kB$; and $q$ is continuous
and strictly decreasing with $q \to 1$ as $\kB \to 0^+$ and $q \to 0$ as
$\kB \to \infty$.
\end{theorem}

The gap between the adaptive and the memorizing policy is exactly half the
information probability, and Coupling B continuously destroys the channel
that makes adaptivity valuable. The theorem is implemented as a unit test:
a trained policy on $E_{2C}$ must trace $J^\ast(\kB)$.

\subsection{Communication denial}

\begin{definition}[Comms denial]
\label{def:comms}
Link $(i,j)$ is alive at $t$ with probability $p_{\mathrm{link}}(\|x_i -
x_j\|)(1-\delta)$, with $p_{\mathrm{link}}$ a range cutoff at $R_{\mathrm{comm}}$.
Messages pass over the realized graph.
\end{definition}

$T_K$ does not depend on $h$: denial is mechanistically independent of the
hazard, even though their values interact (Remark~\ref{rem:voc},
Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}).

\subsection{Protocols, the compositional gap, and nested ablations}

\begin{definition}[Training protocols and the compositional gap]
\label{def:gap}
Let $J_\theta(\pi)$ be the expected episodic return of $\pi$ in
$\mathcal{M}_\theta$ and $\thetastar$ the test configuration (all elements
active at a held-out severity). $\Theta_{\ISO}$ is the set of configurations
with at most one of $\kA, \kB, \delta$ nonzero. The \ISO{} protocol trains one
policy on a uniform mixture over single-element configurations; the \JOINT{}
protocol trains one policy on a mixture whose support includes the all-on
configuration at training severities. The compositional gap is
$\Gam(\thetastar) = J_{\thetastar}(\pi_{\JOINT}) - J_{\thetastar}(\pi_{\ISO})$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[An irreducible shift term for \ISO]
\label{prop:shift}
For any policy and configurations $\theta, \theta'$ sharing $R$ with $|R| \le
R_{\max}$, $|J_\theta(\pi) - J_{\theta'}(\pi)| \le \frac{\gamma
R_{\max}}{(1-\gamma)^2}\,\varepsilon(\theta,\theta')$ with $\varepsilon$ the
supremum over states of the $L_1$ distance between the transition kernels
plus the maximal $L_1$ distance between observation kernels. For every
$\theta \in \Theta_{\ISO}$, $\varepsilon(\theta, \thetastar) \ge
\varepsilon_{\min} > 0$, attained on states where a collapse-seeded fire is
within attenuation range of an agent.
\end{proposition}

The proposition bounds what can be guaranteed, not what happens; compositional
generalization can and sometimes does emerge from isolated training. Its role
is to locate where \ISO's guarantee breaks---the \emph{coupling-co-active}
region---and thereby what to measure: visitation of that region and failure
inside it, which the environment logs from the first step.

\paragraph{Nested ablations are exact.} Every element is a parameter of one
kernel: $\beta$ in $T_H$; $\kA$ in $T_H$ through its dependence on $c'-c$;
$\kB$ in $O^i$; $\delta$ in $T_K$. Setting one to zero recovers the nested
model \emph{bitwise}: every stochastic branch consumes its random stream
unconditionally, comparing against a probability that may be zero, so
trajectories under $\kA = 0$ are identical to the uncoupled model's under the
same keys. A test asserts this, and committed trajectory digests fix it
across refactors. Ablations therefore carry no confound from
re-implementation or re-tuning.

\paragraph{The concrete instance.} A $64 \times 64$ grid, $12$ agents,
horizon $256$, five actions, a foraging task with $32$ items and $+1$ team
reward per item collected. Each agent observes a $9 \times 9$ egocentric crop
with seven indicator planes plus smoke, attenuated per Definition~\ref{def:coupB},
and an own-state vector with absolute position and time. The policy is a
shared-parameter actor-critic (two convolutions, one dense layer, two heads)
trained with independent PPO; the environment, rollouts and training are all
jitted JAX with time as a scan and agents as a vmap. Locked constants:
$\kA = 0.06$, $r_A = 1$, $f_{\mathrm{weak}} = 0.15$, $\lambda_0 = 5\times
10^{-5}$, $\lambda_{\mathrm{load}} = 4 \times 10^{-4}$, $\kB = 1.0$,
$\sigma_s = 1.0$, $\eta = 0.5$, $\delta = 1.0$ when on, $R_{\mathrm{comm}} =
16$. Every locked constant lives in a machine-readable registry that a test
asserts against the configurations, so a constant reachable only from a
command-line flag fails the suite.
