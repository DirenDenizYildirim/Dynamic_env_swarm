\section{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:repro}

The anonymized supplementary material contains the environment, training,
evaluation and calibration code, every configuration, the machine-readable
registry of locked constants with the test that asserts it, the frozen
analysis plan and the four registered outcome framings with their dates, the
unblinding instrument with its synthetic-data tests, the pinned toolchain
(Python~3.12, JAX~0.11.0; the bitwise-nesting certification was made on it),
and the per-run provenance of every grid run (card, commit, timing, archive
hash). Committed trajectory digests fix the environment's behaviour across
refactors. Appendix~\ref{app:floors} lists every measured floor with its
card, run length and artifact.
