   prediction at JOINT (+0.033, SEC-4) against Γ₄ and Γ₄′; SEC-3 against
   RES-7. Labelled non-verdict-bearing, uncorrected; knees UNDERPOWERED;
   mediation VOID. Exists.
4. **Training-mixture composer (new; §1, §9).** ISO / ISO-4 / JOINT as stacked
   shares (δ-only i.e. fire-only, A-only, B-only, A+B), with per-element
   marginals and the active share beneath. Configs only (DES-7, DES-8,
   DES-14). Display only, no predictive slider. Effort S.
5. **Coupling A self-limitation (Fig 3; §5).** Severity toggle; seeded
   ignitions 4.05 / 3.41 / 0.84 (CA-4, m44) with m35 beside (CA-5); flat
   near-agent share (CA-6); the Prop. 3 handshake as a separate panel (CA-2
   and CA-3 are two runs). No rate fit.
6. **Co-active distribution (Fig 5; §5–6).** Per-severity histograms,
   P(zero) (CO-2); κ_B arms toggle. Distribution, never the mean alone;
   recorded, not graded; co-active only in all-on configurations.
7. **Floors explorer (Fig 7; §8).** Cards, run lengths, artifacts (METH-1,
   METH-2, METH-5, HW-1); both confounds on the figure (card × code tree;
   length × box).

## Builder's corrections (against the record)

   deviation". Reversed: k = 60 is the registered ladder's cap and its
   prefix is flagged UNDERPOWERED; **k = 72 is the recorded deviation**
   (DES-1, DES-3; RES-3).

   "both nonzero differences in ISO-4 against JOINT". Wrong: **the active
   share is matched** (1 and 1); only the per-element marginals differ
   (0.5 vs 1.0, 2×) (DES-14).

   restyle agent was told it may use a plain policy label. Whether labels
   appear, and whether ISO/JOINT GIFs appear at all, is the owner's decision
   (GIF slot 2).
