### G The interaction check in full

The interaction check of §6.4 is post hoc. It was proposed after the registered result was known and was fixed in a written analysis plan before any of its evaluations ran. The plan was amended once, still before any evaluation ran, after a review of this paper's draft questioned its design; the amendment added the fifth condition, the rules for the headline and its context, the logit-scale view, the verification mode and the public timestamps described below. The analysis script refuses to run unless the plan is committed and unchanged and its hash matches the one the evaluation run recorded, so a later edit to the plan shows as a refusal. The plan also fixes that its hash is timestamped publicly before the run, and the results' hash after it; this shows, outside our own records, that the plan existed before the run's recorded start, though not that the run began no earlier. The supplement carries no version history, so its verification mode checks the plan's hash against the run's record and recomputes the report from the shipped evaluations (§8). The check never joins the confirmatory family and changes no registered reading.

Four of the conditions are θ\* with only $\kappa_A$ and $\kappa_B$ changed: neither coupling (both 0), Coupling A only ($\kappa_A = 0.06$<!-- N:CA-1 -->, $\kappa_B = 0$), Coupling B only ($\kappa_A = 0$, $\kappa_B = 1.0$<!-- N:CB-1 -->), and both, which is θ\* itself. The fifth, links intact, is θ\* with only δ changed, from 1.0<!-- N:COM-6 --> to 0. β = 0.49<!-- N:CAL-3 --> and every other fixed value stay at θ\*'s. The four new configurations are generated from θ\*'s file, and a test asserts that each loads to θ\* with exactly its κ or δ values replaced.

The policies are the final checkpoints, at update 1000<!-- N:DES-13 -->, of every ISO and JOINT run (72 seeds each<!-- N:DES-3 -->) and every ISO-4 run (20 seeds<!-- N:DES-13 -->), each archive verified against its recorded sha256 before use. Each policy is evaluated in each condition on the registered draw, 512 episodes from evaluation seed 0<!-- N:DES-13 -->, with actions sampled as trained. Because every condition uses the same seed, each policy meets the same initial states and random streams in all five, and the conditions differ only through the couplings and the links. That gives 820<!-- N:IC-1 --> main evaluations and 45<!-- N:IC-2 --> more for the self-floor. The arms met the five conditions differently in training: ISO trained on each coupling alone with links intact and on neither coupling with links denied, ISO-4 only on each coupling alone with links intact, and JOINT only with every stressor on, all at β = 0.43 and 0.70<!-- N:CAL-3 -->, never at 0.49<!-- N:CAL-3 -->. No arm trained with both couplings on and the links intact: ISO and ISO-4 met intact links only with one coupling at a time, and JOINT never did. This is why the arm rows are reported beside the pooled one.

For policy $i$, $J_i(c)$ is its mean completion or survival over the episodes in condition $c$, $I_i$ is the contrast displayed in §6.4, and $D_i = J_i(\text{links intact}) - J_i(\text{both})$. An arm's estimate is the mean over its seeds, with the standard error from their spread, which carries both seed and episode-sampling variation. The pooled estimate weights each arm by its number of seeds and does not assume that the arms share one value. $I$ and $D$ each form a family of three arms and the pool on two co-primaries, eight<!-- N:IC-4 --> intervals, Šidák-corrected within the family (z ≈ 2.73<!-- N:IC-5 -->); the intervals are normal, as for the registered contrasts and Γ₄. Each estimate's detection limit is its 80 %-power MDE from the observed standard error<!-- N:METH-7 -->. Differences between arms are reported without a test. Outside both families and with no reading rule, the report also gives each arm's five condition means; the main effects $J(\text{A only}) - J(\text{neither})$ and $J(\text{B only}) - J(\text{neither})$, which show whether each coupling alone moves the trained policies at θ\*; and $I$ recomputed on the logit of the condition means, which shows whether the reading depends on the rates' ceiling. A policy with a condition mean of exactly 0 or 1 has no finite logit and is left out of that view and counted.

For the first-seed checkpoint of each arm, every condition is evaluated three<!-- N:IC-3 --> more times on the run's GPU, so each checkpoint and condition has four<!-- N:IC-3 --> copies. The analysis reports whether the copies are bit-identical, their spread per metric and the spread of $I$ and of $D$ across copies. Each condition is floored separately: the earlier finding that evaluations at θ\* reproduce exactly on one GPU (§8) is not assumed for the other conditions. If any copy differs, a detectable reading also needs the estimate to exceed, in absolute value, twice the largest floor spread of its own contrast, the post hoc two-sigma floor-grading convention. The "both" evaluation of every policy is also compared, descriptively, with the original evaluation of the same checkpoint at θ\*, and the report states how many agree on both co-primaries.

An estimate of $I$ or $D$ shows a detectable effect if its Šidák interval excludes zero and the floor check is met; its sign and size are then reported, for survival also in agents per episode (× 12<!-- N:ENV-6 -->). Otherwise it is an exclusion with its bound: $\lvert I\rvert < B$ (or $\lvert D\rvert < B$) at the family-corrected level, where $B$ is the larger absolute end of the interval, with the detection limit beside it. The pooled pair is the headline only if the three arms' readings agree in sign and detectability; otherwise the headline is that the answer depends on how the policies were trained, and the pooled estimate is reported only as an average over the arms' different exposures. Every reading is reported beside the main effects of the two couplings and beside Γ, in the same units, so a small effect reads as small; no smallest effect of interest is set. If the floor, the comparison with the original evaluations or Γ's record cannot be read, the analysis refuses rather than report part of a family. What the check is blind to is listed in §6.4; the arms' training conditions are given above.
