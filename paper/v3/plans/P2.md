## 0. The decisions in brief

1. **Nine figures in the main text, four in the appendix, five v2 figures
   dropped** (§3, §6). The main-text set follows the environment-first
   order: the episode, the step, severity, Coupling A, Coupling B, then the
   worked example (what each arm trained on, the interaction check, the
   plane decomposition, the registered readings).
2. **Two environment properties get no figure:** the hazard-blind reward and
   the bitwise nested ablations. A listing plus the test that enforces it
   proves each one better than a picture (§2).
3. **The interaction check gets one new figure** (§4): a 2 × 2 condition
   plot per arm beside a forest plot of I with per-seed points and the
   measured self-floor. It is built only after the results return, and
   prints no number without a VERIFIED row.
4. **The existing simplex serves for the SEC-1 plane decomposition** (§5).
   It needs a label rebuild ("knee", "no-element", `sweep_c50_p000`), and,
   as a proposal, its rows reordered so that γ_p and γ_n come first.
5. **The printed figures are illegible because they print small, not
   because the page lacks room** (§1.3). In the v2 PDF every companion
   prints about 231 pt (3.2 in) wide. Its 7–8 pt labels print at about
   2–4 pt. The fix is a print-size rebuild of the companions, printed at the
   page column. That is more than a label rebuild, so it is a proposal (§7.2).
6. **An iframe costs nothing in print; its companion and caption cost all
   of it** (`paper/v2/src/kit_styles.css:9-13` hides iframes in print). The
   figures in v2's main text cost **3.46 printed pages of 33.4** (§1.2). So
   the 12–15-page target (V3-S1) is mainly a text problem. The figure set
   should still stay at or below about 3.5 printed pages.
7. **Every rebuild writes to a new output directory.** v2's build reads
   `paper/figures/interactive/out/` and `paper/figures/out/` at build time
   (`paper/v2/src/build_v2.py:70-73`). Rebuilding in place would silently
   change the v2 record (§9).

### 1.3 Why the printed figures are illegible (blind review W9, :489)

- Every companion is embedded as an **800 px image at 245 ppi**
  (`pdfimages -list`, all 15 images). It prints **222–255 pt wide**
  (renders of pp. 2, 3, 8, 22, 29), narrower than the 296 pt text column.
  The kit's `figure.html` serves 480/800/1400 px WebP variants
  (`KIT_NOTES.md:159-163`), and the print picked the 800 px variant. Why the
  image does not fill the column is **unexplained**; B2 should check.
- The companions were drawn for screens. The source widths are
  9.2–10.4 in for the interactive companions (`static_companions.py:304,
  423, 626, 762, 914, 1123, 1221, 1392`) and 6.0–6.1 in for
  `fig04`–`fig06` (`paper/figures/src/fig04_coupling_b.py:54`,
  `fig05_coactive.py:40`, `fig06_comms.py:63`). The labels are 6.5–9 pt.
- **Effective printed size of an 8 pt label** = 8 × 3.27 in / source width.
  That gives **2.5 pt** for the 10.4 in companions (ladder, simplex,
  severity, floors, Coupling A, schematic), 2.6–2.8 pt for Γ(t), the
  composer and the explorers, 3.5 pt for the opening ladder, and about
  4.3 pt for `fig04`–`fig06`. All are well below a readable print size.
- Printed at the page column (≈ 372 pt, 5.2 in), a 10.4 in companion's 8 pt
  label would still print at about 4 pt. **Widening alone does not fix
  legibility.** Re-laying the companion out at print size does (§7.2).
- No v2 check grades printed legibility. The kit check "print companions
  shown in print" passed (`paper/v2/out/kit_checks.md`, Print group) while
  every label was unreadable.

## 4. The interaction-check figure (V3-E1; built only after the results return)

**What exists to draw from.** PLAN.md (`che/bench/results/phase6/post_v3_interaction/PLAN.md`;
`paper_v3_prompt.md:315-332`) is **not yet written** (the directory does not
exist on 2026-10-14). The figure is designed against the outputs that the
prompt's step 0.2 requires. The file names and the interval level are
whatever PLAN.md and the analysis script fix. Until then they are
**unknown**, and this plan does not guess them. Those outputs:

- per seed, per policy arm (ISO 72, JOINT 72, ISO-4 20), per condition
  (neither, A only, B only, both), per co-primary: the eval mean J, on the
  registered draw with common random numbers;
- per seed: I = J(both) − J(A) − J(B) + J(neither);
- per arm and pooled: the mean of I and its interval on seed dispersion,
  at PLAN.md's family-corrected level;
- the **measured eval self-floor on the run GPU**: the dispersion of I
  itself across repeated evaluations of fixed checkpoints (A-vs-A,
  CLAUDE.md "floors are also PER-ARTIFACT"). PLAN.md must say whether the
  floor is per arm. If it is not, the figure draws one band and says which
  artifact it came from.

**Layout (one figure, two rows: survival, then completion).**
- **(a) Condition means: a 2 × 2 interaction plot, one small panel per arm.**
  The x-axis is Coupling A off/on, with two lines, Coupling B off (solid)
  and on (dashed). The mean J is plotted. Parallel lines mean no
  interaction; the gap between the lines at "A off" is B's own effect at
  θ\*. This panel shows the professor's point 1 (log:6125) directly, as
  data, with no words. The positions are plotted data. A printed mean
  needs a row, and none is printed by default.
- **(b) I by arm, as a forest plot.** One row each for ISO, JOINT, ISO-4,
  and then pooled. Each row has jittered per-seed points (faint) and the
  estimate with its PLAN-level interval. The existing marks are used:
  `DESIGN.md:159-169`, a hollow marker for a non-registered estimate, a
  1 px muted zero rule, and a stipple band for ±1 sd of the self-floor,
  centred on zero. An MDE wash appears only if PLAN.md computes one.
- **Factual labels only:** "I = J(both) − J(A only) − J(B only) + J(neither)",
  "evaluated at θ\*", the arm names with k, the interval level, and a
  **"post hoc" chip**, styled like the simplex's "secondary" chip. There is
  **no verdict chip**. "No detectable interaction" is a reading rule for
  the text, not a registered string (FIGURES FIRST allows registered
  strings only, log:5874). There is no cross-arm difference of I: PLAN.md
  tests per arm and pooled, and the figure does not invite more.
- **Interactive:** a metric switch, hover or focus on a seed point showing
  its four J values and its I, and the table view (`<details>`). No
  animation.
- **Print companion:** both rows and both panels, drawn at print size (§7.2).

**Text and caption (V3-S4).**
- **The caption says what the figure shows.** A sketch: the four
  conditions' means per arm, and the interaction contrast I per arm and
  pooled, with per-seed values, intervals and the evaluation self-floor.
- **The paragraph that introduces the figure states, once:**
  - that the analysis is post hoc;
  - that it was planned and committed before its run, and stays outside
    the registered family;
  - what it is blind to:
    - fixed final checkpoints, so it measures how the couplings combine on
      trained policies' outcomes, not whether training composition helps;
    - θ\* only, since Low and High were not run (log:6230-6237);
    - δ held at θ\*'s value;
    - as a double difference, it cancels additive effects by construction;
  - the bound, if no interaction is detected.
- **Labelling conflict (owner question 6).** The prompt says that post hoc
  work is labelled "wherever it appears" (`paper_v3_prompt.md:63-65`). The
  figure's chip satisfies that. The caption names "the interaction
  contrast", but repeats no limit.

**Numbers.** The figure prints the estimate and interval of I for each arm
and pooled, for both co-primaries, and the self-floor sd. Each gets a
VERIFIED row in `paper/v3/ledger_proposals/` before it is shown, routed
through `paper/figures/interactive/src/ledger.py` with `SHOW_PENDING` off
until then (`interactive/MANIFEST.md:9-15`).

**Build.** A new builder, `build_interaction.py`, and its companion
function. They follow the existing pipeline (`common_web.py`, `ledger.py`,
`check_outputs.py` for the double-blind scan), write to the v3 output
directory (§9), and read only the committed analysis outputs and
PLAN.md's committed constants. They compute no new estimator.

   `sweep_c50_p000` (`build_simplex.py:182, :280`). Fixes are in §7.1.

   then γ_p, γ_n and the bound (`build_simplex.py:105-155`). For SEC-1 to
