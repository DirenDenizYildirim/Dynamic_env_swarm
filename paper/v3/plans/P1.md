## 0. The argument in six sentences (planning prose, not paper text)

1. CHE is the contribution: a pure-JAX multi-agent environment where a
   cellular-automaton fire burns every episode, collapse seeds fire
   (Coupling A), smoke attenuates perception (Coupling B), communication
   denial is an independent axis, the reward never reads the hazard, and
   every stressor is a parameter that switches off to a bitwise-identical
   nested model (§1 below).
2. Its calibration says what each stressor does, at which severity, and how
   finely that can be measured (floors).
3. The worked example is the pre-registered test of training on
   co-occurring versus isolated stressors; its registered readings are
   reported verbatim, and the paper says plainly that its emphasis moved to
   the environment after the result was known (V3-F5; log:6167-6169).
4. The example's central limitation is stated first: it was sited where
   composition is weak, a limitation registered for every outcome in
   advance (PFB:245-260), which the post hoc interaction check measures.
5. The result, sized plainly: about a tenth of an agent per episode, visible
   only late in training, and it does not survive the inert-share
   correction (RES-2, RES-12, DX-406 → NEW row N1).
6. The lesson depends on the interaction check (§3.3); a check-independent
   lesson always holds: active share = μ_A + μ_B − p, so matching the
   active share cannot isolate co-occurrence (V3-F4; BR:83-99).

### 5.2 V3-F4: identity, plane, test, future work
